Tungkol sa amin

Isang matatag at maaasahang barangay para sa lahat

Ang Barangay AdReiNo ay may malapit na relasyon sa komunidad at naghahatid ng mga serbisyo na nakatuon sa safety, welfare, at dignidad ng bawat residente.

Misyon: Gawing mabilis, transparent, at maayos ang public service sa bawat pamilya at purok.

  • Transparent at accountable governance
  • Accessible services for every resident
  • Community-focused programs and public safety

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request barangay clearance?

Visit the barangay hall with your valid ID and required documents. You may also ask the front desk for the checklist.

Can I ask for assistance for medical needs?

Yes. The health center and barangay office can help with health consultation, referrals, and community support.

What should I bring when filing a complaint?

Bring a valid ID, relevant statements, and any evidence or witness information to help with the report.

When is the barangay office open?

Monday to Friday, from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. For urgent cases, emergency hotlines are available.
